FAQS

Frequently asked Question & Answers Here

What services does the Fire Department provide?

-Firefighting and Rescue: Responding to fires, controlling hazards, and conducting search and rescue operations.
- Emergency Medical Services: Offering first aid, basic life support, and pre-hospital care.
- Fire Prevention and Safety Education: Inspecting buildings, enforcing fire codes, and running public awareness programs.
- Hazardous Materials Response: Managing chemical spills, gas leaks, and other dangerous substances.
- Disaster Response: Assisting in natural disasters such as floods, storms, and building collapses.
- Community Support: Providing fire safety training, drills, and emergency preparedness workshops.

How do I report a fire or emergency?

To report a fire or emergency, contact our emergency response line immediately. Provide your location, the nature of the incident, and any important details so our team can respond quickly.

Can I request a fire safety inspection for my property?

Yes. You can request a fire safety inspection for your property by contacting our team directly. We will schedule a visit to assess fire risks, check compliance with safety standards, and recommend the right preventive measures.

How can I obtain a copy of a fire incident report?

You can obtain a copy of a fire incident report by contacting our office with the date, location, and details of the incident. Once verified, we will provide you with the official report for your records.

What fire safety equipment do you provide for homes and businesses?

We provide a full range of fire safety equipment for both homes and businesses, including fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, fire alarms, sprinkler systems, fire blankets, emergency exit signs, and personal protective equipment. Our team also ensures proper installation, testing, and maintenance of all equipment.
